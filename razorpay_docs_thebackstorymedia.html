Sole Proprietorship Model
Prepared for: NPVL Executive & Management Board KYC Activation: Fast-Track (24–48 Hours)
Instant Onboarding Flow: NPVL Sole Proprietorship status ke anusaar yeh sabse saral (simplest) documentation checklist hai. Isme kisi complex audited financial statement ya CA certificate ki aavashyakta nahi hai. Niche diye gaye 4 basic documents submit karte hi account instant live ho jata hai.
1. Proprietor KYC (Individual Proofs) Mandatory
  • Proprietor PAN Card Clear photo / scanned copy (Proprietor ka personal PAN card).
  • Proprietor Aadhaar Card Front & Back copy (Mobile number linked hona chahiye OTP verification ke liye).
2. Business Proof (Only 1 Required) Easiest & Free
  • Udyam / MSME Registration Certificate Recommended: udyamregistration.gov.in se 10 min mein free download ho jata hai (Aadhaar + PAN se).
  • GST Certificate (Optional) Agar GST number registered hai toh de sakte hain (mandatory nahi hai).
3. Bank Account Proof (Payouts & Settlements) Settlement
  • Cancelled Cheque ya Bank Statement / Passbook First Page Account Holder Name, Account Number aur IFSC Code saaf dikhna chahiye.
  • Account Type: Firm Current Account ya Proprietor ka Savings Account (initial KYC ke liye dono valid hain).
4. Official Business & Contact Info Account Info
  • Official Business Email ID Jisme Razorpay account login banega aur payment reports aayenge.
  • Official Mobile Number OTP verification aur transaction alerts ke liye.
  • Registered Office Address Full address with Pincode (website compliance ke liye zaroori).
