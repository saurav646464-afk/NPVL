NPVL?
What is NPVL?
NPVL is envisioned as a professionally structured volleyball league focused
on creating a competitive platform for athletes and building a stronger
volleyball ecosystem across North India.

NPVL aims to:

Create a professional platform for volleyball

Provide athletes with structured competitive opportunities

Connect grassroots talent with higher-level competition

Build strong regional teams and identities

Create opportunities for brands, sponsors and franchise owners

Take North Indian volleyball to a larger national audience

33 Investment 	&

Revenue 	Model
A 	Sustainable League Economy
NPVL is built on a shared-value model where the league, franchises and partners grow together.
01
THE 	LEAGUE
Owns central rights: media, title & associate
sponsorships, licensing and digital. Revenue
flows into a central pool.
02
THE 	FRANCHISE
Invests through the franchise fee and team
operations; earns a share of central revenue plus
its own team-level income.
03
THE 	PARTNERS
Brands invest in sponsorship and integration
packages in return for visibility, engagement and
association.
